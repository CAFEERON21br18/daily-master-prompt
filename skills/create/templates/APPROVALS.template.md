# Approvals

Actions a session wants to take but needs your permission for. Write `approved` or `denied` (with a reason if you like) under **Decision:**. Nothing here is done until approved.

Entry format:

```
## A-NNN · YYYY-MM-DD · pending
**Action:** what the session wants to do
**Why:** what it unblocks
**Risk / cost:** what could go wrong, what it costs
**Decision:**
```
